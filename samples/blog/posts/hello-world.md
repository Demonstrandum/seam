Welcome! This site is generated by [seam](https://github.com/Demonstrandum/seam),
from a handful of `.sex` files and some Markdown posts like this one.

Every page shares one layout, written once as a macro in `site.sex`.
Each page passes its own details to it with `%with`, which is how this page
knows its title is "(%do %title)".

There are (%length %posts) posts so far.
